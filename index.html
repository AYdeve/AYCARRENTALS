About AY Car Rentals

Who We Are

Driven by Excellence

AY Car Rentals was founded in 2020 with a simple but powerful mission — to make car rental in Nigeria easy, affordable, and reliable for everyone.

What started as a small fleet of 5 cars in Akure has grown into a nationwide service with over 50 vehicles across Lagos and Akure. We serve business travellers, families, event planners, and everyday Nigerians who need a dependable ride.

Every vehicle in our fleet is regularly inspected, fully insured, and cleaned before each rental. We don't just rent cars — we give you peace of mind on the road.
